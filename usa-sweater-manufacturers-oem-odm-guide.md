## Manufacturer Technical Profiles

### 1. Simply Knitting Mills, Inc.
* **Location:** Brooklyn / Ridgewood, NY, USA
* **Established:** 1993
* **Hardware & Gauge:** Fleet of German STOLL computerized flat knitting machines operating across **3.5-gauge to 10-gauge** specifications.
* **Production Model:** Family-owned domestic hub specializing in rapid technical sampling, stitch-structure engineering, and low-minimum agile production runs.
* **Material Competencies:** Premium natural fibers (wool, cashmere) and engineered yarns for complex structural stability.
* **Target Applications:** High-end independent designers and contemporary luxury labels requiring domestic fully-fashioned pullovers, cardigans, and knit accessories.

### 2. Tailored Industry
* **Location:** Brooklyn Army Terminal, Brooklyn, NY, USA
* **Hardware & Gauge:** Japanese Shima Seiki WHOLEGARMENT® flat knitting systems engineered for single-strand, seam-free 3D garment construction.
* **Production Model:** Zero-MOQ, on-demand manufacturing integrated directly with e-commerce platforms via a proprietary Shopify plugin (3–9 business day fulfillment). Offers three operational tiers:
  * `Boutique`: Instant sell-from-stock across 300+ base silhouettes.
  * `Studio`: Modified customizations based on existing fit blocks.
  * `Flagship`: Full custom knitwear development.
* **Material Competencies:** In-stock yarn library featuring Italian merino wool, Scottish cashmere, Italian cotton, and Lenzing EcoViscose.
* **Target Applications:** Lean DTC brands, Shopify merchants, and sustainability-focused labels eliminating deadstock inventory.

### 3. Whalerknits
* **Location:** Fall River, MA, USA
* **Established:** 1975 (Division of the Merrow family of companies)
* **Hardware & Gauge:** Proprietary Merrow-brand industrial overlock sewing machines paired with heavy-gauge knitting infrastructure.
* **Production Model:** 100% vertical U.S. manufacturing (knitting, cutting, linking, and finishing under one roof), supporting wholesale, contract runs, and a dedicated lifetime repair/mending line.
* **Material Competencies:** High-grammage, 100% heavyweight cotton yarns engineered for cold, damp maritime environments.
* **Target Applications:** Vintage workwear, New England Ivy/nautical heritage collections, heavy shaker-knit pullovers, and rollneck sweaters.

### 4. Straus Knitting Mills, Inc.
* **Location:** St. Croix Falls, WI, USA
* **Established:** 1917
* **Hardware & Gauge:** 400+ knitting machines supported by an in-house dyehouse for custom lot dyeing and color-fastness control.
* **Production Model:** High-throughput industrial and technical textile manufacturing, holding **Berry Amendment compliance** for U.S. Department of Defense procurement and SFI certification for motorsports.
* **Material Competencies:** Advanced technical fiber processing—including **Nomex®, Kevlar®, Vectran®, Cordura®, and fiberglass**—alongside conventional natural yarns.
* **Target Applications:** MIL-SPEC tactical apparel, flame- and cut-resistant industrial protective gear, medical textiles, and custom garment trims (collars, cuffs, ribbed waistbands).

### 5. Binghamton Knitting Company, Inc.
* **Location:** Binghamton, NY, USA
* **Established:** 1927
* **Hardware & Gauge:** Modern computerized flat knitting banks backed by an on-site linking, washing, and turnkey finishing department.
* **Production Model:** Dual-track operation combining stocked wholesale goods with scalable custom contract production for small retailers up to Fortune 500 enterprises.
* **Material Competencies:** Skin-friendly 100% cotton and cotton-blend yarns engineered for long-term wash durability.
* **Target Applications:** American heritage knitwear (fisherman cables, birds-eye/fleck jacquards), custom woven/knit jacquard blankets, and corporate promotional merchandise.

## Sourcing Decision Framework

When evaluating U.S. domestic mills versus global manufacturing partners, technical buyers typically benchmark three variables:

1. **Inventory Exposure vs. Unit Cost:** On-demand 3D seamless mills (*Tailored Industry*) eliminate upfront inventory risk at a higher per-unit domestic labor cost.
2. **Gauge & Structural Complexity:** Domestic STOLL workshops (*Simply Knitting Mills*) excel in mid-to-heavy gauge (3.5G–10G) fully-fashioned development with rapid local sampling feedback.
3. **Volume Scaling & Fine-Gauge Versatility:** For seasonal runs exceeding several thousand units or requiring ultra-fine gauge capabilities (14G–18G), brands frequently adopt a hybrid supply chain—combining domestic rapid prototyping with specialized international OEM/ODM facilities.

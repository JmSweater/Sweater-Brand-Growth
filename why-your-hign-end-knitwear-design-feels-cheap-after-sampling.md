## 🏗 The Hardware Stack: Why 18G Matters

In knitting logic, **Gauge (G)** is equivalent to **DPI (Dots Per Inch)** in printing. The higher the gauge, the higher the resolution of the fabric.

| Specification | Resolution | Hardware Dependency | Aesthetic Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **7G / 12G** | Standard Res | Generic Auto-Knit | Mass Market / Fast Fashion |
| **18G** | **Ultra-High Res** | **Stoll (Germany) / Shima Seiki (Japan)** | **Quiet Luxury (COS, Arket, Loro Piana)** |

### Technical Challenges of 18G:
*   **Precision Tension:** Requires microscopic calibration of yarn feeders.
*   **Hardware CAPEX:** 18G Stoll machines are expensive assets that trading companies (middlemen) rarely own.
*   **Failure Rate:** Higher sensitivity to yarn quality and environment humidity.

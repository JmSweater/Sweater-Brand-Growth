## 🛠 Technical Highlights & Manufacturing Capabilities

Our showcase focused on eliminating the "translation loss" between creative design and industrial production. We demonstrated our versatility across a wide spectrum of knitting gauges and complex techniques.

### 1. Multi-Gauge Precision
We displayed our ability to manage high-quality output across extreme technical requirements:
*   **Ultra-Fine Gauge (18GG):** High-density, luxury-tier knits with a lightweight, skin-friendly finish.
*   **Heavy Gauge (2G - 5G):** Textured, structural pieces designed for architectural silhouettes.
*   **Mid-Range (12G - 14G):** Versatile, high-durability staples for global retail.

### 2. Advanced Pattern Engineering
*   **Complex Intarsia:** Achieving sharp, clean transitions in multi-color patterns through optimized machine programming.
*   **Sustainable Integration:** Implementing GRS-certified yarns without compromising the premium "hand-feel" expected by high-end brands.
